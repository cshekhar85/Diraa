Chapter 00 · Framework Overview
00 / FRAMEWORK

How to use the tracker

Each section lists what to track, threshold levels that matter, and how each segment transmits to others. The final section maps complete cause-effect chains. India-specific overlays are marked [IN].

01 · REGIMEStart with Fed expectations, 10Y real yield, DXY, VIX and credit.

Chapter 01 · Fed / Rates
01 / THE MASTER VARIABLE

Fed / Rates

The Master Variable. Rates set the global discount-rate and policy backdrop.

InstrumentWhy It Matters
US 2Y Treasury YieldMost policy-sensitive rate; moves with Fed Funds expectations
US 10Y Treasury YieldGlobal equity discount rate; P/E benchmark for all risky assets
US 10Y TIPS Real YieldMost important number in the tracker — primary gold driver
2Y–10Y Yield CurveInversion = recession signal (Estrella-Mishkin model)
3M–10Y Yield CurveBest historical recession predictor; watch <0 bp threshold
Fed Funds Futures (CME)Implied rate path 6–12M forward; market's bet on the Fed
SOFR OIS CurveNear-term rate expectations; replaces Eurodollar futures
Break-even Inflation (5Y, 10Y)Nominal yield minus TIPS real yield; inflation expectations
Real yield < 0%Gold bull case structurally valid
Real yield > 2%Peak pressure on gold, growth equities, EM
2s10s < 0 for >3MRecession probability >60% within 12–18M
Fed Funds futures pricing >100 bps of cutsReflationary equity environment
INDIA OVERLAY [IN]
  • RBI Repo Rate (current policy rate)
  • India 10Y G-Sec yield (sovereign benchmark)
  • India–US 10Y differential (ideally >300 bps for FPI bond comfort)
  • RBI OIS 1Y (domestic rate expectations)
India rate transmission
US rate hike ↓ INR under pressure ↓ RBI forced hawkish ↓ G-Sec yields up ↓ Nifty P/E compress

How Fed / Rates drives everything else

→ DXY: Hike cycle → DXY ↑ (rate differentials widen vs G10)
→ Gold: Real yield ↑ → Gold ↓ (strongest inverse; near-mechanical)
→ Equities: Discount rate ↑ → P/E multiple contracts; growth stocks hit hardest
→ High Yield Credit: Rate ↑ → refinancing cost ↑ → High Yield spreads widen
→ EM/India: Hike → capital flows to USD assets → FII outflows from EM

THE CREDIT LEAD RELATIONSHIP
High Yield spreads lead equity market stress by days to weeks. This is the most reliable early warning signal in all of intermarket analysis.
Standard sequence
MOVE ↑ ↓ CDX High Yield widens ↓ 5–10 day lag ↓ Equities sell off
CDX High Yield SpreadRegime
< 300 bpsExtreme compression; high-yield investors taking risk
300–400 bpsNormal; credit markets healthy
400–500 bpsMild stress; watch for widening trajectory
> 500 bpsStress regime; equity drawdown likely beginning
> 700 bpsRecession/crisis pricing
INDIA OVERLAY [IN]
  • India has no deep public High Yield market. Use proxies:
  • NBFC vs Bank bond spread (systemic shadow banking stress)
  • AAA corporate vs G-Sec spread (credit risk premium)
  • PSU bond spreads
  • Commercial Paper (CP) 3M rates (short-term corporate liquidity stress)
  • 2018 IL&FS, 2020 Franklin Templeton — both showed in CP/NBFC spreads weeks before equity hit

THE COPPER / GOLD RATIO — LEADING INDICATOR
Copper/Gold ratio leads US 10Y Treasury yield by 4–8 weeks. Ratio rising → expect bond yields to rise → equity re-rating pressure. Ratio falling → expect bond yields to fall → gold supported; growth equities benefit.

India crude impact transmission [IN]

Every $10/bbl rise in crude
approximately $14B/year increase in India's import bill.
India transmission
1. Current Account Deficit widens ↓ 2. INR under pressure ↓ 3. Domestic inflation rises ↓ 4. RBI hawkish bias; rate cuts delayed or reversed ↓ 5. G-Sec yields rise ↓ 6. Nifty P/E multiple compresses

Chapter 09 · Transmission Chains
09 / CAUSE → EFFECT

Intermarket transmission chains

Tap any chain below to expand its step-by-step vertical pathway.

Chain 1: The Rate Hike Cycle (Tightening Regime)
1 Fed hikes interest rates
2 Real yields rise
3 DXY strengthens
4 Gold falls
5 Commodities fall
6 High Yield credit spreads widen
7 Equities lag and then sell off
8 EM capital outflows begin
9 INR weakens
10 Imported inflation rises in India
11 RBI forced hawkish
12 G-Sec yields rise
13 Nifty P/E multiple compresses
Chain 2: Risk-Off Cascade (Shock or Black Swan Regime)
1 VIX spike or Black Swan event
2 Credit spreads blow out (CDX High Yield widens sharply)
3 Equity sell-off follows credit by 5–10 days
4 Dollar demand surges as safe-haven bid
5 DXY rises
6 EM capital flight begins
7 INR falls
8 FII aggressively sell Indian equities
9 Gold rises if real yields are simultaneously falling
10 Oil falls as demand destruction is priced in globally
Chain 3: China PMI Inflection (Growth Recovery Signal)
1 China PMI beats expectations and turns upward
2 Copper rallies
3 Iron Ore rallies
4 Brent follows
5 AUD/USD rises
6 DXY faces pressure, weakens slightly
7 EM equities rally broadly
8 India Metal and Mining stocks outperform next session
Chain 4: Fed Pivot / Rate Cut Cycle (Reflationary Regime)
1 Fed signals pivot to rate cuts
2 Real yields fall
3 DXY weakens
4 Gold rallies strongly
5 Commodities rally
6 EM inflows accelerate
7 INR stabilises and strengthens
8 FII buy India
9 Equity P/E multiple expands
10 Small and mid cap leads large cap outperformance
11 High Yield credit spreads tighten
Chain 5: Oil Shock (India-Specific Transmission)
1 Crude rises sharply due to geopolitical event or OPEC supply cut
2 India CAD widens by approximately USD 14B per USD 10 rise in Brent
3 INR weakens
4 Domestic fuel price inflation rises
5 CPI pressured upward
6 RBI delays rate cuts or is forced to hike
7 G-Sec yields rise
8 Banking sector faces NIM pressure and bond mark-to-market losses
9 DII rotates to defensives: FMCG, Pharma, IT
10 MCX Gold rises in INR terms even if COMEX is flat
